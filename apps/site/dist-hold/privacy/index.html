Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

NovaProse is an independent open-source project. The novaprose.com website and the NovaProse Cloud waitlist are run by the project’s founder as a private individual (the data controller). For any question about your data, write to hello@novaprose.com.

What we collect

Cloud waitlist. Your email, role, team size, site language, the time you joined and the version of the consent text. We don’t store your IP address: it is only kept in server memory to limit how many sign-ups come from one address.

Collaboration demo. Text, comments and the name you enter in the demo are visible to other people in the same room. Demo rooms are not saved: they are removed from server memory when the last person leaves.

Browser settings. Your theme and demo name are kept in your browser’s localStorage and are not sent to us.

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies and don’t load third-party trackers.

Why and on what basis

We use the waitlist email only to tell you about NovaProse Cloud early access and its terms. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). We don’t sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Where data is stored

Waitlist entries are stored on our server in Helsinki (Finland, EU), hosted by Hetzner. The website is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site.

How long we keep it

While the waitlist is open and for no longer than 12 months after Cloud launches — or until you ask us to delete it or withdraw your consent.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time — write to hello@novaprose.com from the email you signed up with. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
